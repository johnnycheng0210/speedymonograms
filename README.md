# Speedy Monograms

The marketing website for **Speedy Monograms** — a custom embroidery and printing
business in Rosedale, Auckland, New Zealand. The site presents the shop's work,
services, and contact details in a timeless, elegant style (deep royal navy, warm
paper tones, and Garamond typography).

## What the site covers

Three pages walk a visitor from an introduction to getting in touch:

- **Home** — an introduction to the shop, its craft, and its services.
- **Services** — the work on offer, with a short description and image for each.
- **Contact** — address, phone, email, opening hours, and a map link.

### Services featured

| Service | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Embroidery** | A professional finish that lasts, stitched to your design. |
| **Vinyl Printing** | Bold, durable prints for names, numbers, and logos. |
| **Photo Transfer** | Full-colour images transferred crisply onto garments. |

## Imagery & branding

- **Logo:** the Speedy Monograms crest (`public/logo.png`), rendered white on dark
  surfaces.
- **Photos:** images of embroidery, printing, and apparel shown across the site,
  held in `public/images/`.
- **Favicon:** a small SVG "SM" mark (`public/favicon.svg`).
- **Look & feel:** deep royal navy and warm paper, set in Cormorant Garamond,
  EB Garamond, and Jost.

## Where the content lives

All business details and the service list are kept in one place — `src/data.js` —
so contact info, hours, and services stay consistent everywhere they appear on the
site.
